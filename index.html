    Questions people ask

    Which pods fit the RELX Infinity 2?

    Both RELX Pod Pro and RELX Pod Pro 2 fit the Infinity 2. The Eco, Smooth and Boost power modes work with Pod Pro 2.

    Does RELX Pod Pro 2 fit the RELX Essential?

    It usually does, but some Pod Pro 2 packs are labeled for the Infinity 2 family only. Check that your pack lists Essential before you buy.

    What is the difference between RELX Pod Pro and Pod Pro 2?

    Both hold about 1.9 mL and fit the same Infinity-shape devices. At PodX, Pod Pro comes 2 pods per pack and Pod Pro 2 comes 1 pod per pack. Pod Pro 2 is the pod for the power modes on Infinity 2 and Infinity 2 Plus.

    Do RELX Pod Pro pods fit the RELX Creator or ULTRA?

    No. Creator and Creator Pro use their own Creator pods, and ULTRA uses its own ULTRA pods, so Pod Pro 2 does not fit either.

    Do RELX Pod Pro pods fit the RELX Classic?

    No. The Classic takes RELXPods (Classic pods) only, while Infinity and Essential devices take Pod Pro and RELX Pod.

    How many puffs does a RELX pod give?

    Usually about 600 to 650 puffs per 1.9 mL pod. Real puffs depend on how long and how hard you draw.
